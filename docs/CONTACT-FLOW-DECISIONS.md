# Contact Flow Decisions

The paid Formspree automatic acknowledgement is intentionally deferred at the owner’s request. Formspree’s official Auto Response action is documented as a Professional or Business feature, so no subscription upgrade or alternate mail provider was added. The visible post-submit confirmation on the site remains active.

The contact form now stores a visitor’s name and email only after they actively opt in and successfully submit a message. The data remains in that browser’s local storage, can be removed with **Clear Saved Details**, and is not sent unless the visitor submits the form.

For Formspree Auto Response availability, see the official guide: <https://help.formspree.io/articles/plugins/sending-a-confirmation-or-response-email>.
